Mambo Dinamico Dance Company

Overview

This case study highlights a series of promotional videos created for Mambo Dinamico Dance Company, featuring themed Valentine’s and Quinceañera concepts. As their videographer and editor, I produced fun, energetic, and visually engaging content designed to promote their community classes, special workshops, and cultural celebrations.

Each video blends Mambo Dinamico’s strong technical foundation with playful, themed storytelling that appeals to dancers, families, and social dance audiences.

‍

Mambo Dinamico

Mambo Dinamico is a choreographer and dance performer from France who has built a global reputation for his unique dance style, stemming from classical ballet and jazz techniques. He first rose to international fame as a finalist on Britain’s Got Talent with his all-male high-heel dance trio. Since then, he has toured worldwide, teaching workshops, headlining performances, and collaborating with major artists including Céline Dion, Mariah Carey, and Beyoncé.

My Skills Applied

My skills used for this project include:

  • Videography: To professionally record Yanis' dance workshop in a creative and informative manner.
  • Video Editing: To select, cut and arrange the raw footage in the post-production process to create a cohesive and engaging final video.
  • Communication: To make sure I film what is intended and do not interfere with the class while I am recording.
  • Camera Framing: To ensure that Yanis and the dancers are in frame in and focus.

‍

Goals

The workshop was designed to teach people how to dance like Mambo Dinamico in heels successfully.

  1. Empowerment: To create a safe, inclusive space where dancers could explore self-expression and confidence through movement.
  2. Balance: To teach people how to dance in high heels without falling.
  3. Choreography: To teach participants the technical precision and artistry of Yannis’ choreography in solo and group settings.
  4. Style: To teach dancers Yannis’ style of dancing through ballet and jazz techniques.
  5. Performance Experience: To give dancers the opportunity to perform choreography in a professional, high-energy environment.

Workshop Experience

  • Warm-Up & Foundation: The session began with a technical warm-up that emphasized alignment, strength, and balance — crucial skills for executing choreography in heels.
  • Choreography Instruction: Yannis taught an original routine that highlighted his signature style: sharp yet fluid movements, dynamic use of musicality, and a balance of sensuality and strength.
  • Performance Coaching: Beyond movement, Yannis emphasized stage presence, emotional connection to the music, and the confidence needed to “own the room.”
  • Community Atmosphere: The workshop fostered a strong sense of support and camaraderie, with dancers encouraging one another while learning challenging material.

‍

Challenges

  • Dancing in Heels: Many participants faced the technical challenge of balancing technique with performance while wearing heels.
  • Pushing Beyond Comfort Zones: Yannis encouraged dancers to step outside of their usual movement styles, demanding both vulnerability and boldness.
